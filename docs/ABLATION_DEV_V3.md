## Reading
- **Nothing is adopted; v2 stays.** Every variant lowers the dev IC.
- The drop is largest for slow-moving fundamentals (0.030 → 0.007) and much smaller for the fast-changing ones
  (0.027). Slow ratios are nearly constant per stock, so with ~100 names they act as a stock identifier and the trees
  learn "which companies did well in the training years" — that does not carry forward. Group means
  (`ind_ret_21/63`) do the same for industries.
- Fundamentals are long-horizon signals (value, quality, growth work over months). The 1–12-month ranking models
  (still mock) are where they belong; the features are built, point-in-time tested and ready for that.
- Observation, not a decision: with all fundamentals the dev top-decile Sharpe rose (1.32 vs 1.09) while IC fell.
  The selection criterion (IC) was fixed in advance, three years of one strategy are noisy, so this is noted only.
- The 2019–2026 test folds were **not** run for these variants — nothing was selected, so there is nothing to test.
